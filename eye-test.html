How to Take the Eye Test

  • Sit comfortably and relax your eyes.
  • If you use glasses or lenses, wear them during the test.
  • Make sure your device screen is clean and brightness is comfortable.
  • Take a clear photo of your eye using the camera or upload a photo.
  • Click "Analyze Image" to get instant results.
  • If the result looks abnormal, please consult an eye specialist.
