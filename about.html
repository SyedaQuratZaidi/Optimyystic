About Us

Our Story

This idea is truly unique. It came to my mind while thinking differently at a vision exhibition. While everyone was busy showcasing their own work, I wondered, what if we create something that benefits everyone? That moment inspired me to combine technology and empathy, and that's how Optimmystic was born.

Inspired by this vision, we set out to create Optimmystic—a place where AI meets empathy to make eye care accessible for all.

  • 2025: The idea for Optimmystic was born!
  • 2025: Team assembled, first prototype launched.
