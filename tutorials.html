Best Practices for Accurate Results

Follow these tips to ensure the most accurate vision assessment

Lighting Requirements
  • Use natural daylight when possible
  • Avoid harsh shadows on your face
  • Ensure even lighting on both eyes
  • Avoid backlighting or glare
Camera Positioning
  • Keep camera at eye level
  • Maintain 18-24 inches distance
  • Center your face in the frame
  • Keep the camera steady
Eye Preparation
  • Remove makeup around eyes
  • Clean glasses if wearing them
  • Look directly at the camera
  • Keep eyes wide open
Quality Assurance
  • Use high-resolution camera
  • Take multiple shots if needed
  • Check image clarity before submitting
  • Retake if image is blurry

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our eye test process

How accurate is the online eye test?

Our AI-powered eye test provides preliminary vision screening with high accuracy for basic vision assessment. However, it's not a replacement for comprehensive eye exams by qualified optometrists or ophthalmologists. We recommend using our test as a screening tool and consulting eye care professionals for detailed diagnosis.

What equipment do I need for the test?

You only need a device with a camera (smartphone, tablet, or computer with webcam) and a stable internet connection. Ensure your camera has good resolution (minimum 720p recommended) and your device supports modern web browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

How long does the eye test take?

The entire process typically takes 3-5 minutes. This includes filling out the patient information form (1-2 minutes), taking the eye photo (1-2 minutes), and receiving AI analysis results (1 minute). The actual image capture and analysis are very quick.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we take privacy seriously. Your personal information and eye images are processed locally in your browser and stored securely. We don't share your data with third parties, and you can delete your test results at any time. All data transmission uses encryption.

Can I retake the test if I'm not satisfied?

Absolutely! You can retake the test as many times as you need. We recommend retaking if the lighting wasn't optimal, the image was blurry, or you want to track changes in your vision over time. Each test result is saved separately so you can compare them.
